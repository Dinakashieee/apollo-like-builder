# Architecture
- Landing-page themes use a shared renderer and scoped semantic tokens for identical editor/public output; legacy templates remain supported without rewriting saved content.
- Campaign landing designs import sequence text explicitly; workspace-owner subscription access is checked by a shared RPC and enforced on design saves and storage writes, so client controls cannot bypass paid access.
- Company signals are generated by the authenticated company-signals edge function and persisted with evidence/status; clients only supply company context, so unsupported scores remain unknown.
- Lead inserts synchronize workspace-deduplicated account records; manual creation, lead imports and SignalHire claims invoke company research after successful saves, avoiding secret-dependent dispatch.
- Company imports and CSV exports use shared company data helpers; CSV exports neutralize formula injection.
